Questions people ask

Will these names work in Free Fire and PUBG?

Most styles work. A few symbols are blocked by some games. If a name shows as boxes or gets rejected, try a style from the Fonts category with fewer symbols.

Why do some letters look like boxes?

Your device font does not include that character. The name is still correct, and it will look right on devices that support it.
